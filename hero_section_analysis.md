## 3. Detailed Component Breakdown

### A. Top Status Badge
* **Purpose**: Sets expectations immediately that full event details and registration are coming soon while prompting immediate email sign-up.
* **Layout**: Centered inline pill container with subtle glow or border.
* **Copy Example**: `COMING SOON — JOIN THE WAITLIST` or `EMBRACE KONGO '27 ✦ COMING SOON`

### B. Hero Headline & Flanking Metadata
* **Main Headline**: `Embrace Kongo '27` (or `EMBRACE KONGO '27`) rendered in a super bold, massive font that anchors the entire page.
* **Flanking Metadata (Left & Right)**:
  * **Left Side**: `Brazzaville, Congo` (Location & Venue indicator)
  * **Right Side**: `July 23 – August 1` (Event Dates)
* **Visual Rhythm**: High contrast between the mega title font size and the crisp, clean, tracking-wide uppercase typography of the flanking metadata.

### C. Subtitle / Value Proposition
* Concise 1–2 line description explaining what Embrace Kongo '27 is, building anticipation before full details drop.

### D. Primary CTA: Integrated Waitlist Email Capture Form
* **Input Container**: A sleek, pill-shaped input bar housing both the text field and action button.
* **Input Field**: Clear placeholder text such as `"Enter your email address..."`.
* **Expressive Action Button**:
  * **Text Options**: `"Join the Waitlist →"`, `"Get Notified for Launch"`, or `"Reserve Your Spot"`.
  * **Visual Style**: Distinct high-contrast background with smooth hover/active state animations.
* **Reassurance Subtext**: Micro-copy directly underneath (e.g., *"No spam. Be the first to get exclusive access when registration opens."*).
